## Version 0.9.0

<en-US>
- The Added receipt only undoes from its Undo button and clears itself after five seconds.
- Tapping the tick on that receipt or the trash icon on the delete bar dismisses the offer without undoing.
- The entry form only points out a missing amount, category or account after you start editing, and in the order of the fields.
- Settings shows a page heading.
- Linden is now licensed under GPL-3.0.
</en-US>

## Version 0.8.0

<en-US>
- Hide Totals now hides every amount in the app, not just the totals.
- An amount typed as a calculation is saved as its result, like 12+3.50 becoming 15.50.
- The undo bar for a deleted entry now appears at the bottom of the ledger.
- Importing a file that is not a valid Ivy backup reports an error instead of deleting your data.
</en-US>

## Version 0.7.0

<en-US>

- System back keeps an unfinished entry instead of clearing it.
