## Full description (max 4000 chars)

Linden is a fast, private expense tracker for keeping your spending under control — with no accounts, no ads, and no cloud.

TRACK EVERYTHING IN ONE PLACE
Log expenses, income, and transfers in seconds. Group entries by account and category, and see exactly where your money goes.

MULTI-CURRENCY BY DESIGN
Hold accounts in CHF, CNY, EUR, GBP, HKD, IDR, INR, JPY, SGD, and USD. Linden converts each total into your default currency using live exchange rates.

FAST, SMART ENTRY
• A built-in exact calculator handles amounts on the fly
• Descriptions and fields are suggested from your past entries
• Transfers between accounts, including cross-currency, in a few taps

PAYMENT CAPTURE (ANDROID)
• Let Linden read payment notifications from the banking apps you choose
• Each payment waits in a compact bar on the Entry screen until you confirm it — nothing is added automatically
• Linden remembers how you classified a payment and prefills the next matching one

UNDERSTAND YOUR SPENDING
• Ledger views by entries, accounts, or categories
• Period navigation to review any week, month, or year
• Search and filter to find anything instantly
• Balances at period end, with future entries handled honestly

YOUR DATA, YOURS
Linden stores everything locally on your device. No sign-up, no ads. Your financial data stays on your device or in your own Android backup; only anonymous usage and crash data help improve the app. Back up and restore your full history to a file whenever you like, or import existing data.

Linden is a personal finance tracker, not a bank. It never connects to your accounts.
